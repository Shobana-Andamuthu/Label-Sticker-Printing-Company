Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about lead times, minimum order quantities, prepress setup, and waterproof substrates.

What is the standard turnaround time for custom labels? +

Standard production turnaround is 24 to 48 hours following digital proof approval. Expedited same-day press runs are available for rush orders.

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)? +

Our minimum order quantity is just 50 units thanks to digital laser die-cutting with zero physical plate setup fees. We also produce multi-million roll runs for enterprise brands.

Are your bottle and jar labels 100% waterproof and oil-resistant? +

Yes! Our White BOPP, Clear PET, and Heavy Cast Vinyl materials paired with high-gloss UV or velvet matte laminates are 100% impervious to water, essential oils, alcohol, and ice bucket submergence.

Do you charge setup or die fees for custom shapes? +

Never! We utilize optical laser converting technology that cuts any custom contour outline directly from your vector cutline with zero tooling or die charges.

How do I get a free sample pack before ordering? +

Click "Request Free Sample Pack" on any page or form. We will mail you a comprehensive sample book containing all 12 commercial substrates and finishing coatings for 100% free with free shipping.

What file formats and color spaces should I submit for prepress? +

We accept vector AI, PDF, EPS, and SVG files, as well as high-resolution (300+ DPI) PSD and PNG files. Artwork should ideally be designed in CMYK or specify Pantone PMS codes. We include a standard 1/16" (1.5mm) bleed check for free.

Can I split my total order quantity across multiple design variations? +

Yes! As long as the label size, substrate material, and finishing coating remain the same, our digital printing press workflow allows multi-SKU versioning across your total order quantity with zero plate change fees.
